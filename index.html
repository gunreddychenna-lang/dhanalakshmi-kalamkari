Authentic Srikalahasti (Sreekalahasthi) Hand-Painted Pen Kalamkari Sarees

Welcome to Kailash Kalamkari — home of original, 100% hand-painted Srikalahasti (Sreekalahasthi) Pen Kalamkari pure silk sarees, dupattas, and fabrics. Located in the sacred temple town of Srikalahasti in Andhra Pradesh, India, our workshop has been preserving the ancient art of visual storytelling since 1984 under master craftsmen.

100% Freehand Pen Kalamkari Art

Unlike machine-printed or block-printed imitations, every motif on our Kalamkari sarees is painted completely by hand using organic bamboo pens (kalam) with zero synthetic chemical prints.

Natural Organic Mineral Dyes

Our colors are extracted naturally from earth minerals, pomegranate rinds, myrobalan seeds, and indigo roots, washed multiple times in the sacred Swarnamukhi river waters.

Pure Handloom Silks & Kanchipuram Borders

We paint on certified pure Kanchipuram silk, Tussar silk, Organza, Gaduwal, and Chanderi fabrics, ensuring heirloom-quality texture, luster, and longevity.
