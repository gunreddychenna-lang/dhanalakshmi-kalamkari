Artisan Authenticity & Shipping Policy

100% Genuine Hand-Painted Art Guarantee: Every piece from Dhanalakshmi Kalamkari is drawn and painted individually by skilled artisans using traditional vegetable dyes in Srikalahasti. Minor irregularities in drawing or color smudges are natural hallmarks of authentic hand-craftsmanship.

Pre-Dispatch Video Inspection: To guarantee complete transparency, we encourage customers to inspect their chosen saree or dupatta live via WhatsApp video call before dispatch.
